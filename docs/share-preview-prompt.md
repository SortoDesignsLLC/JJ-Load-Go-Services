# Share preview artwork

Created using the built-in ImageGen tool, with the existing business poster and truck photo as references. The generated master is kept outside the repository; the optimized 1200 × 630 JPEG is in `public/social/jj-load-and-go-preview.jpg`.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing / compositing.
Create a beautiful finished website social link-preview banner for JJ Load & Go Services. Wide landscape aspect ratio 1200:630 (roughly 1.905:1). This is the artwork itself, no mockup.
Input 1 is their existing business brand poster: reference for exact spelling, red/black/white brand character, and the distinctive large italic red JJ lettermark with white and black outline. Extract and faithfully preserve the JJ lettermark, but do NOT reproduce the full dense poster, its tiny services or phone number. Input 2 is their authentic red Chevrolet pickup and wooden-sided black utility trailer photo: preserve that vehicle, trailer, their actual geometry and appearance. Use the actual photo as the photographic focal point, do not invent another truck, front grille or people; natural cropping is fine.
Composition: premium local hauling brand with commanding but restrained typography. Matte near-black background, clean red edge accent. Left approximately 48 percent: the JJ lettermark and a crisply designed white condensed bold italic wordmark reading exactly "LOAD & GO", with "SERVICES" in small spaced capitals beneath. One supporting line near lower left: "WE HAUL IT ALL." Right approximately 55 percent: the authentic red truck and trailer photo, tastefully integrated with dark vignette into the black on left, preserving realistic red paint and scenery. The large JJ / LOAD & GO grouping should fill the left half confidently, with beautiful spacing. Think polished automotive brand campaign, not a flyer.
Colors: deep black charcoal, authentic scarlet truck red, pure white text. Strong light/dark contrast. No gratuitous shine or flares. Essential lettering within 7 percent safe margins on all edges. Large readable branding at 300px thumbnail width.
Exact visible text only: "JJ", "LOAD & GO", "SERVICES", "WE HAUL IT ALL." No phone, URL, extra claims, service lists, buttons, stars, browser frame, watermark or unrelated branding.
